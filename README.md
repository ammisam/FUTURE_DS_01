# FUTURE_DS_01: Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Internship Details
- **Organization:** Future Interns
- **Domain:** Data Science & Analytics
- **Task:** Task 1 – Business Sales Performance Analytics
- **Project:** Sales Store Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail store sales transactions to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and operational issues.

The objective is to turn sales data into useful business insights that can support better decisions, improve customer experience, and increase profitability.

## 2. Business Problem
The store needs better visibility into its sales, popular products, customer preferences, revenue-generating categories, and order cancellations.

Without proper analysis, the business may miss sales opportunities, make poor inventory decisions, experience increased operational costs, and struggle to improve customer satisfaction.

## 3. Project Objectives
- Identify the top five products by quantity sold.
- Analyze products with frequent cancellations.
- Identify the time of day with the highest number of purchases.
- Find the top five highest-spending customers.
- Identify product categories generating the highest revenue.
- Calculate return or cancellation rates by category.
- Analyze preferred payment methods.
- Explore purchasing behavior across age groups and genders.
- Examine monthly sales trends.
- Identify opportunities to improve business operations.

## 4. Dataset
**File:** `sales (1)(1).csv`

The dataset is used as the source for retail sales transaction analysis. It should be inspected for its actual columns, data types, missing values, and duplicate records before analysis.

## 5. Tools and Technologies

## 7. Analysis Approach
1. Review the dataset and understand its structure.
2. Check data quality and clean records where necessary.
3. Use SQL queries to investigate the business questions.
4. Analyze product sales, revenue, customers, and order status.
5. Examine purchasing times and monthly sales trends.
6. Interpret the findings and identify business improvement opportunities.

## 8. Key Business Questions
- Which products sell the most?
- Which products are frequently canceled?
- When do customers purchase most often?
- Who are the highest-spending customers?
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- What are the cancellation rates across categories?
- Which payment method is most preferred?
- How do age group and gender relate to purchasing behavior?
- How do sales change from month to month?

## 9. Business Recommendations
Recommendations will be based on the results of the completed analysis. Potential areas for improvement include inventory planning, cancellation monitoring, customer targeting, promotional strategies, and operational efficiency.

## 10. Conclusion
This project applies data analysis techniques to retail sales data to investigate business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

The final conclusions and numerical findings should be documented after validating the SQL query results.
